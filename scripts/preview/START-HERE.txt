Recipes By Ninjas - local preview

In Terminal, paste these one at a time (if your zip is in Downloads):

  cd ~/Downloads && unzip -o recipes-by-ninjas-preview.zip
  cd ~/Downloads/recipes-by-ninjas
  python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1

Then open http://localhost:8000. Press Control + C in Terminal when done.

Preview notes: Ask Ninjas answers from the recipe book only (no AI here). Sign-in, synced favorites and emails don't run in this preview. Photos load from the internet, so stay online.
